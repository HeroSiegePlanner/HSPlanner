## New features

- Size stats (Area of Effect, Explosion AoE, Orbiting Skill Size, Melee Attack Range, Projectile Size and the rest) show a Hit area diagram, like Path of Building's AoE preview: the main skill's real collision masks from the game, drawn to scale over an Act 1 town screenshot at field of view 1.0 (training dummies 72 px apart) at their base size and with your build, with the reach of spinning objects and the skill's hit radius checks. It lists which size stats reach the skill and how much each adds to its scale, following the game's rules: Area of Effect only for Area of Effect skills, half of melee attack range for melee skills, Fire Nova and Whirlwind taking a reduced share, Spacial Conversion and Concentrated Damage removing the radius they convert; stats that do not reach the skill are named. Explosions a skill spawns grow with Explosion AoE whatever the skill's tags, as the game's explosion code adds it; the game's target rings (the AoE indicator before a cast, the flash where it lands) are drawn at their fixed size; and allocated passives whose objects carry the skill's size stats (Oops, Unstable Bomb, Shadow Anomalies, Whiplash, Cheapshot, Combat Order) are drawn with it.
- Projectile Speed stats show a Flight diagram beside the Hit area one: the main skill's projectile at its base speed and with your build over the same Act 1 town view, animated side by side with a dot every 0.1 s and a mark where it vanishes, plus each moving object's speed in px/s, range and lifetime. Speeds, lifetimes, the 1500 px limit and which objects take the bonus come from the game. It follows the game's rule: the percent applies first, the flat amount is added after it and never multiplied, and a total at or below zero is skipped, so Chronobreakers never slow a projectile; chain lightning style projectiles take only the percent. Melee and Ranged Projectile Speed from the Incarnation tree count only for Melee + Projectile and Ranged + Projectile skills, and stats that do not reach the skill are named.
- Gear has "Suggest upgrades…": slot by slot it tries other items and every item modification (gems, jewels and runes in sockets, runewords, satanic crystals including the extra socket, the chest's angelic augment, unholy random affixes, stars and the rolls a new item lets you pick) and keeps only what raises Combined DPS in the full calculation, or Hit DPS when "Rank by" is set to it, so other skills and ailments can't outweigh the main skill's hits. Each step names the item and lists its changes and its gain on top of the steps above it, and the summary shows Hit damage, Hit DPS and Combined DPS as the stat panel does, now and with every change. Apply takes every step up to that one and Apply all takes them all, as one undoable edit. A second pass tunes the items again once the rest of the gear has changed. Affixes and crystals assume their maximum roll and augments their top level with the Config toggle on; a relic is never worn twice, your own items keep their modifications unless that kind of change is enabled, and charms, potions and the mercenary stay as they are.

## Interface

- Dialogs share one layout: a header naming what the dialog is about, a body that scrolls between a header and footer that stay in view, widths by purpose and the close button with Escape as the only way out (footer Cancel, Close and Done buttons are gone). Save as, Rename, New folder, the changelog, keyboard shortcuts, stat sources, skill subtrees and both text editors now match Settings and the update dialog, and dialogs with typing in them no longer close on a stray click outside.
- The build library no longer shows a Season column or a build's season, and builds no longer store one: saves and build codes from an earlier season load with their trees as they are.

## Calculations

- Flat and percent Projectile Speed are now separate stats, as in the game: "+2 to Projectile Speed" no longer adds to "+15%". Sharpshooter's Cloak, Leviathan's Ribcage, Chronobreakers, Overloader, Wave Length and every subtree speed node give percent; Flying Debris and Slug Slinger give +1 flat Melee or Ranged Projectile Speed instead of +1%; Titans Power is its own multiplier.
- Angelic augments count the way the game applies them. Damage augments (e.g. Butcher's Fury, Staticshot, Magefury) are their own proc hit, switched on under Procs, instead of adding their damage to the character; timed buffs and conditional bonuses (e.g. Lethal Tempo, Berserker's Rage, Artful Dodger) apply while switched on under Item Blessings, and buffs that proc less often than they last count at their uptime; always-on augments stay as stats. Tempest Strikes, Monstrosity and Avariciousness stack under Combat stacks (Monstrosity's full-stack burst counts for its share of the fill cycle), Magistriker scales with attacks per second, Drunkard Mayhem with active buffs, Minion Mastery speeds up summons instead of the hero, Survivor's Shield counts its immune share of each cooldown, and Zapper (switched on under Procs) adds the lightning its electrocuted targets pass on to nearby monsters. Values, triggers, durations and proc cooldowns follow the game (Weapon Throw, Berserker's Rage, Warsong, Corrosion, Nordic Winter and the spell procs' damage were wrong), spell procs gain their "+X% damage per level of this augment" synergy, and Elementalist now scales with every 100 maximum mana. Longevity raises damage and duration of orbiting skills only, Titan's Power counts its 2 s of every 30 s, Echoes of the Void recasts the skill every 30 s (faster with kills per second), Nordic Winter adds damage, Infinity adds multicast chance, Corrosion stacks enemy damage taken under Combat stacks (10 seconds by default), and Gravity and Warsong reduce damage taken — these augments previously had no effect on the numbers.
- Crushing Blow is calculated. Each proc puts a stack on the target that lasts 3 seconds (a new proc adds one and refreshes the timer), up to 5 stacks plus Maximum Crushing Blow Stacks, and each stack breaks floor(10 × (1 + Crushing Blow Effectiveness / 100))% (12 with one Crusher, 15 with two). The chance rolls once per damage object or projectile and rounds up like the game's integer roll (10.5% acts as 11%). By default the stacks are the average that the main skill's hits per second keep up; Config → Combat Stacks can set the count instead. The stacks join Armor Break (Sundering Strikes included) in one physical break sum that multiplies only the physical part of a hit, after resistance: elemental skills and an attack's converted elemental damage gain nothing. Physical hits of non-attack skills now take Armor Break as well; before, only attacks did. The Stats panel shows Maximum Crushing Blow Stacks and Crushing Blow Armor Break with their sources.
- Attacks lose the fixed 1.5 physical multiplier the planner used to add (the old "crushing blow default"). As in the game, a physical hit takes 1 + (Armor Break + Crushing Blow)/100, so an attack's physical damage without any break is a third lower than before. Converted elemental damage copies the hit before that break: it loses the 1.5 too and no longer gains from Armor Break.
- Physical damage converted to an element (Fire, Cold, Lightning, Poison and Arcane Impact and their small nodes) follows the game. Only the element with the largest total conversion applies, ties going to arcane, cold, fire, lightning, then poison, and the other conversions do nothing; sources of the same element add up. The converted share leaves the physical hit and becomes that element, scaled by its own Skill Damage: 1000 physical with Cold Impact and +40% Cold Skill Damage hits for 500 physical + 700 cold. The converted part takes the element's resistance and breaks, Crushing Blow and Armor Break reach only the physical rest, and skills without physical damage gain nothing. Magic Skill Damage and Total element Skill Damage no longer raise converted damage.
- Ignore Resistance stops at 100% for spells and other non-attack skills too, as it already did for attacks, so stacking it past 100% no longer multiplies damage. Stats show it as 100%, and its breakdown lists the raw total as Overcap (#205).
- Item stats that convert one stat into another now count: Intelligence, Vitality, Energy and Armor converted to Magic Skill, Attack or elemental Skill Damage, Cold and Poison Resistance converted to their Skill Damage, Faster Cast Rate converted to Movement Speed and Movement Speed converted to Total Damage Dealt each add their share of the source, like the Incarnation tree's conversions (#206).

## Skills

- Mercenaries no longer have Aura Expert, a Hero Siege 1 skill the current game removed, and points a saved build put into it no longer count as spent (#210).

## Incarnation tree

- Feast of the Elements and Vital Resistance turn resistances into Increased Life instead of flat Life, and their Life breakdown names the resistances they read (#209).

## Items

- Grimwalkers matches the game: [320-400]% Enhanced Defense, +[30-40] to All Attributes, [30-40]% Magic Skill Damage and +40% Movement Speed instead of fixed or inflated values. Flo's +2% goes to Maximum All Resists, Nju's 3% joins the runeword's -[10-15]% to All Enemy Resistances, Skill Movement Diminish decreased by [40-80]% is added and the stray +64 Defense is gone. Its Demon's Presence proc (level 15-20) appears under Item Blessings in Config. Runewords can now roll ranges, item tooltips list runeword stats in their own Runeword section instead of under Implicit, From Sockets names a runeword's runes without repeating the stats it already includes, and Projectile Speed shows as a flat value (#204).
- St. Tomi's Vibrant Aura: Angel's Vibrance gives percent Increased Life and Mana Replenish instead of flat replenish (#207).
- Every runeword matches the game: its stats roll their real ranges (Etherwalk [220-300]% Enhanced Defense, +[1-2] to All Skills, [20-30]% Movement Speed), missing stats, skill bonuses and auras are added, and stray ones such as the boots' +64 Defense are gone. Weapon runewords roll their higher two-handed values on two-handed bases, and runeword procs such as Etherwalk's 3-6% chance on cast to cast Mana Devour are listed in the Runeword section. Rune bonuses were corrected: Old gives +15% Enhanced Defense, Uth 5% of Damage Taken Recouped as Mana, Sal 4% Physical Damage Taken Reduced, Ber +1% Deadly Blow and 8% Deadly Blow damage, and Drax +3% Maximum Weapon Damage (#208).
- Small, Large and Grand Charms roll their own affixes as in the game, with each size's tier ranges and names (to Life rolls [2-10] on a Small Charm, [5-20] on a Large and [10-30] on a Grand at tier 1), instead of one list shared with other gear. Charm affixes that added the wrong stat now add the one they name: percent Life and Mana, Life and Mana per second, Replenish Life and Mana, flat skill damage and Explosion AoE, and enemy resistance affixes have values at every tier. A charm rolls one element, so its resistance, skill damage, additive damage and enemy resistance affixes share it, and Small Charms take up to 3 affixes, Large 4 and Grand 5. Charms in saved builds keep their affixes.
