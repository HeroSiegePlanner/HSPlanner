## Season 11

- Class skills take the newer patch notes' damage, synergy and Intelligence scaling, cooldowns and subskill values.
- Skills the notes tag [Hybrid], such as Inferno Slash, Homing Missile and Shield Wall, also take Spell nodes and Spell-only bonuses, and the listed Spell and Strike tags are added or removed.
- Angelic augments use their new proc chances and values, and Lethal Tempo and Wizard's Wrath stack up to 3 times, counted at their average stacks.
- Satanic and set weapons roll the new Enhanced Damage ranges, and the listed relics, charms, amulets and affixes take their new values.
- Blender keeps 40% of other area bonuses, Slicing Throw takes half the size bonus, and Charged Bolts and Lightning Surge fly faster.
- The Strange Encounters ether node gives 8%.

## Interface

- New build opens a dialog to pick the name, class and level, and files the build in the folder you are viewing.
- Import opens a dialog that fills in a build code from the clipboard, previews its class, level and contents, and lets you name the build before it is saved to the folder you are viewing.
- Share codes carry the build's name, so an imported build keeps it.
- Share opens a window with two choices: copy the build code, with or without the stash, or store the build on hsplanner.app and copy its link.
- Import accepts hsplanner.app links as well as build codes.
- Scorching Aura's subskill tooltips use the game's stat names and explain how Flaming Fissure and Ground Blaze hit (#237, #239, #240).
- A tagged tree node's tooltip lists your skills and ticks the ones it benefits, like the game.

## Calculations

- Sub-skill ranks from Peg Leg, Overloaded Dice or Storm in a Vial keep raising Scorching Aura's subskills past their highest rank (#238).
- Those granted ranks now also raise the dummy hits of the other skills' subskills (#238).
- Burn Them All! burns for its Conversion share of Scorching Aura's damage each second; its Burning Damage line, which the game never reads, no longer counts (#239).
- Avatar of Fire grants flat Fire Skill Damage, as in the game, instead of a percentage (#242).
- Elixir of Death's Berserker's Rage raises Attack Damage and Magic Skill Damage, as in the game, instead of total damage (#243).
