## Season 11 (partial patch notes)

- Skill data follows the first Season 11 patch notes: skill damage, synergies and Intelligence scaling, subskill values and maximum ranks for every class with listed numbers, the swapped subskill positions (icons move with their nodes) and the new Spell/Projectile/Ranged tags. Bug fixes the planner already assumed (nodes that "never worked") need no change; Brutalizing Cleave's 225% per rank and Dune Warrior's corrected damage follow the fixes.
- Incarnation tree: Gigantus, Furious Smash, Rapid Mending, Vital Evasion, Vital Heart, the Life Steal big node, Light In The Dark, Vital Resistance, Vital Suppression, Manahunger, Spacial Conversion (50%), Colossus (0.5% per stack), Citadel (40% per stack) and Missile Explosion (any explosion) use the new values. Ether tree: Back to Basics 30%, Ready to Pop 15%, Reaper's Toll -85%.
- Items, runewords and item skills: the listed nerfs and buffs (Fury of Tarethiel, Eternity, Rakhul's Legion Crusher, Dragon's Blessing, Grief, Commander's Sentry Blaster, Tactical Flak Jacket, St. Aaron's Eternal Rage and the rest). St. Neri's Rainbow Lance converts 20% of Lightning Resistance into Lightning Skill Damage. Celestial Might converts half of each elemental skill damage bonus to arcane. Light Radius and Intelligence Added as Magic Skill Damage no longer scale with star levels, and Spectral Scatter no longer gains star ranks.
- Mechanics: Magic Find diminishes with a power of 0.8 (was 0.9); Physical and Magic Damage Reduction diminish past 40% (power 0.85); DoT frequency diminishes past 50% (power 0.75); Jagged Gauntlet lowers critical chance by 25% instead of halving it; Blender gets a third of every other area of effect bonus (Increased and More) while its own Industrial Sized counts fully; Maelstrom of Frost hits every 0.8 seconds.
- Not in yet: new items, Tarot cards, the Meteor, Homing Missile and Benediction subskill trees, new Incarnation and Ether nodes, rearranged subskill trees and changes the notes give without numbers (e.g. Shockwave's area scaling, projectile speeds, St. Soloyolo's Holy Bow), Lightning Fury's spell critical strikes and Blazing Trail's spell duration.

## Interface

- Report a bug has an "Already reported" tab listing the reports tracked on GitHub, with open/closed state and search by title or #number; click an entry to read its description, with a link to its GitHub page.
- Sending a report that looks like an existing one shows the similar reports first, each readable in place; Send anyway sends it, and editing the title or description checks again.
- The gem, rune, runeword, affix and skill pickers in the gear editor look like the item list: a title says what you are picking (e.g. "Pick Gem or Rune for Socket 1") instead of the Items and Stash buttons, groups read "• Gems", and icons fill their slot. Hovering a gem or rune shows an item-style card with its kind, tier and socketed stats.

## Fixes

- "+X to Melee, Ranged, Area of Effect and Orbit Skills" on items now raise the ranks of skills with that tag; before, they did nothing.
- Mana Costs Decreased on items and the Manaleak–Manahunger affixes now reduce mana costs instead of doing nothing or adding flat Mana.
- Gurag's Soulgem gives Life Replenish; its stat was misnamed and did nothing.
- Large negative damage stats (Belt of Skulls' -666% Magic Skill Damage, Lucifer's Crown's damage per resistance point with -300% all resistances) bring a hit down to zero instead of making damage and DPS negative.
- Satanic crystals on satanic, heroic, angelic and unholy charms now count; before, only crystals on equipment did, so a Flat Life crystal on a charm left Life unchanged. Potions no longer offer a crystal slot, as they cannot take one (#214).
- Powerfunnel and Powerfunneled Lifespan convert 1% and 7.5% of the Attack Damage stat to Increased Life; they used to read the weapon's damage after Enhanced Damage and came out about seven times too strong (#215).
- Citadel's Increased Life Replenish per Colossus stack adds to the same pool as other Increased Life Replenish (e.g. Angel's Vibrance Aura) instead of multiplying it again (#216).
- Jagged Gauntlet (St. Ahto's Diamond Hands) works for spells and attacks and grows with its rank: each hit has its chance (4% per rank) to deal extra damage equal to your critical damage, raised by Jagged Gauntlet Damage (10% per rank), while your critical chance is halved and critical damage is capped at 40% + 20% per rank. Before, it only added critical chance, which spells never read. The proc formula follows the ability text, not traced game code (#217).
- Roll the Dice (High Roller's Deathcrown) turns on by itself once the crown's six rolled stats add up to more than 777, stars and pinned rolls included; the Config switch for it is gone (#218).
- Scorching Aura deals damage in the calculation. Before, it was never counted because it is an aura, not an active skill. As in the game, it pulses every 61 frames (about once a second) whatever your cast speed, hitting each enemy once per pulse. Rapid Burning shortens the pulse instead of adding cast rate (16 frames at rank 5). Increased Damaging Aura Effectiveness multiplies the pulse. Sixt Degree Burns and Searing Burst raise its subtree multiplier instead of fire skill damage, and Burning Soul adds 6% per rank for every 100 maximum life to that multiplier. With Scorching Aura as your aura, its DPS adds to your skills' Combined DPS, it is listed under Active skills in the sidebar (× clears the aura slot) and it has its own card among the per-skill DPS. A build that plays only Scorching Aura, with no active skill picked, shows its DPS and leads the Stats view with it.
- Runeword stats with a range can be set like item rolls: each ranged stat of the socketed runeword has its own slider under Stat Rolls, the calculation uses the chosen value instead of the whole range, and the item tooltip shows it. Changing the runeword clears the old values.
- Text edit shows the item's runeword and its stats under "Runeword:"; writing a single value with [custom] on a runeword stat sets that roll.
- Drax and the runewords made with it give Maximum Weapon Damage, as in the game, so they raise weapon attacks only; before, they added damage to spells too (#223). Hydrangea's added minimum and maximum damage is weapon damage too and no longer reaches spells.
- Avatar of Fire gives Spell Critical Chance, as in the game, instead of Critical Chance (#221).
- Scorching Aura is a Spell skill, as in Season 11, so Spell bonuses such as Manahunger's 25% more damage and spell critical chance reach it. Temporal Echo still does not repeat it, as an aura pulse is not a cast (#222).
- Sorting items by DPS and the Incarnation tree suggestions count Scorching Aura; with the aura as your only damage, every item used to show a gain of 0 (#220).
