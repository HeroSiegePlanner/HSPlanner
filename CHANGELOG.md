## New features

- Loadouts replace whole-build profiles: the Incarnation tree, Ether tree, gear and skills each have independent loadouts that can be switched, duplicated, renamed, deleted and undone. Saves and build codes keep every loadout, and existing profiles are converted with their original snapshots kept for recovery. Compact selectors sit in each view's header or tree toolbar, with the other actions in a clearly visible menu.
- Interface language: English, Korean, Russian or Simplified Chinese, applied immediately and saved.
- Equipped potions count in the calculations: a potion that is on in Gear applies its buff, with no separate toggle in Config. Elixir of Death gives +27–39% Damage, Empty Bottle of Vodka +1–5 All Skills, and Sarcaster's Coffee Mug +60% Movement Speed, matching the game.
- Weapon choices follow the game's class rules. Viking, Marksman, Nomad, Redneck, Samurai, Amazon, Demon Slayer, Marauder, Shield Lancer and Butcher cannot equip Wands, Books, Spellblades or Flasks; Witch's Wand and The Absence of Constraint (Unbound Power) lift that block. Mercenaries hold only their role's weapons (Knight melee, Archer ranged, Magister spell; Novelty for all). Off-hand weapons follow each class's dual-wield list (Sword, Dagger, Mace, Axe, Claw, Gun), with Hercules Grip and Master of Wands still adding their weapons. Changing class or mercenary removes weapons that no longer fit.
- Skills check the game's weapon requirements (75 skills, including Wand classification, the Zeal/Berserk subskill exceptions and Shield Slam's extra melee requirement). A skill the weapon cannot cast deals no DPS, spends no mana and triggers no procs. Wargod (737) lifts those requirements and keeps its total damage penalty.
- Custom Config opens in its own editor ("Edit custom stats"): one stat per line, validation with line numbers, and a searchable list of every stat that inserts a line on click. Lines starting with // (or the rest of a line after //) are notes, kept with the build and its build code. Changes apply on Save. Custom stat names appear as tokens with a hover card showing the build's current value (#193).
- Build actions (…) has "Copy build code with stash": the code then also carries the build's stash items, and importing it restores them. The plain Share button still leaves the stash out (#192).
- Stats can be pinned with the star next to each stat. Pinned stats appear in their own panel at the top of the Stats page, follow the search, and are saved for every build (#199).
- Incarnation tree nodes can be excluded from Suggest Nodes: Alt-click a node to mark it with a red ring, and the optimizer will neither allocate it nor path through it. Alt-click again to allow it. Exclusions are saved with the build (#164).
- Relic proc buffs (e.g. Skull Axe's Demon Form, Shiv's Live by the Sword) appear under Item Blessings in Config and apply when toggled; Live by the Sword adds 28% + 1% per level Attack Speed and Movement Speed. Equipping such a relic turns its buff on, and editing the same relic keeps your own setting (#157, #158).
- New Config controls: Fuel to Fire's consumed-at-cast count (each object adds 1% to that buff's damage bonus), Ramping Pulse stacks (5% radius per stack, up to ten), Agitation stacks (movement speed per stack, up to ten), Colossus and Combat Mitigation stacks, and an enemy that is 100% immune to damage over time.
- Gem, jewel, soulgem and rune pickers group by kind and show each socketable's stats.
- The update dialog shows your version, the new one and its release notes as a readable list. Downloads show progress, a failed check or install can be retried, macOS replaces the app and restarts it, and Windows now finds its installer.
- Page Up / Page Down scroll the planner panels (including Stats), the build library and the notes preview.
- Size stats (Area of Effect, Explosion AoE, Orbiting Skill Size, Melee Attack Range, Projectile Size and the rest) show a Hit area diagram, like Path of Building's AoE preview: the main skill's real collision masks from the game, drawn to scale over an Act 1 town screenshot at field of view 1.0 (training dummies 72 px apart) at their base size and with your build, with the reach of spinning objects and the skill's hit radius checks. It lists which size stats reach the skill and how much each adds to its scale, following the game's rules: Area of Effect only for Area of Effect skills, half of melee attack range for melee skills, Fire Nova and Whirlwind taking a reduced share, Spacial Conversion and Concentrated Damage removing the radius they convert; stats that do not reach the skill are named. Explosions a skill spawns grow with Explosion AoE whatever the skill's tags, as the game's explosion code adds it; the game's target rings (the AoE indicator before a cast, the flash where it lands) are drawn at their fixed size; and allocated passives whose objects carry the skill's size stats (Oops, Unstable Bomb, Shadow Anomalies, Whiplash, Cheapshot, Combat Order) are drawn with it.
- Gear has "Suggest upgrades…": slot by slot it tries other items and every item modification (gems, jewels and runes in sockets, runewords, satanic crystals including the extra socket, the chest's angelic augment, unholy random affixes, stars and the rolls a new item lets you pick) and keeps only what raises Combined DPS in the full calculation, or Hit DPS when "Rank by" is set to it, so other skills and ailments can't outweigh the main skill's hits. Each step names the item and lists its changes and its gain on top of the steps above it, and the summary shows Hit damage, Hit DPS and Combined DPS as the stat panel does, now and with every change. Apply takes every step up to that one and Apply all takes them all, as one undoable edit. A second pass tunes the items again once the rest of the gear has changed. Affixes and crystals assume their maximum roll and augments their top level with the Config toggle on; a relic is never worn twice, your own items keep their modifications unless that kind of change is enabled, and charms, potions and the mercenary stay as they are.

## Interface

- The left stat panel is easier to scan: it is slightly wider, values use fewer decimals and no brackets so labels stay on one line, Combined DPS is the only highlighted result, the skill rank shows its allocated and bonus split, eHP sits in its own group under Defense, and removing an active skill uses a dedicated × button.
- The stat panel keeps showing the last results while the same build recalculates, instead of going blank.
- The Stats page title, filters and search stay in place while the list scrolls. Gear item and tree actions sit in toolbars.
- The main window no longer opens partly off-screen at 125% display scaling on Windows, which hid the title bar and close button. The app starts maximized and restores to a size that fits the monitor's work area.
- The gear picker's close button discards an unsaved selection like Cancel.
- Dialogs share one layout: a header naming what the dialog is about, a body that scrolls between a header and footer that stay in view, widths by purpose and the close button with Escape as the only way out (footer Cancel, Close and Done buttons are gone). Save as, Rename, New folder, the changelog, keyboard shortcuts, stat sources, skill subtrees and both text editors now match Settings and the update dialog, and dialogs with typing in them no longer close on a stray click outside.

## Calculations

- Skill DPS follows the game's DPS cards for the training dummy: how many hits one use lands, the uptime of timed buffs and the repeat period of leaps, charges and dashes. Skill coefficients of every class were refreshed from the game.
- Attribute conversions follow the game: 6.5 life per Vitality, 4.5 mana per Energy and 0.1% critical damage per Dexterity; life and mana replenish gain 0.125 per Vitality and 0.175 per Energy without per-attribute flooring. Life, mana and other floored totals are floored even without percentage bonuses. Strength grants 1% Attack Damage per point instead of Enhanced Damage; unarmed Wargod still removes it.
- Attack and cast speed follow the game's formulas. Increased attack speed adds to the weapon rate on at most 0.75 attacks per second (diminishing above 400%, power 0.85), and faster cast rate adds FCR/100 casts per second to each skill's own cast speed (mostly 1.75, diminishing above 350%, power 0.75). Attack-path subtree speed bonuses (e.g. Sun Gods Chosen, Marksmanship) multiply that skill's final attack rate instead of adding to attack speed.
- Defensive caps follow the game: maximum resistance stops at 90% even with +max resistance, physical and magic damage reduction cap at 75% (raised by +max reduction), and weapon-panel critical chance caps at 95%.
- Critical strikes use the pool of the hit's damage type: physical skills crit on attack critical chance and damage, elemental skills on spell critical chance and damage, regardless of the Spell tag. Attacks keep attack crit on their converted elemental share.
- Enemy-side damage follows the game: subtree "enemy damage taken" (e.g. Weakening Blast) joins the elemental break sum instead of multiplying separately and no longer affects physical hits; the low-life bonus is a separate multiplier for physical damage only; resistance penetration is no longer clamped at 100%.
- Cooldowns: Skill Haste recovers 0.5% per point, and subtree cooldown reductions (e.g. Timeless Tundra, Ephemeral Flurry, Blenderang) shorten the cooldown itself (base × (1 − reduction%)) instead of acting as haste. Negative changes are kept, and a reduced cooldown under 0.5 s from a longer base becomes 0.51 s. Cloud of Sand keeps its no-haste exception, and mana sustain respects weapon and cooldown cadence.
- Spell projectile counts include Extra Spell Projectiles % and Additional Spell Projectile for the 44 skills that use them in the game (e.g. Frozen Boulder, Orb of Frost, Charged Bolts): floor(count × (1 + %/100)), then the flat bonus.
- Mana costs: raw mana coefficients and full-rank indexing were corrected for 47 skills. Increased mana cost and mana cost reduction share one pool in the game's order, keep fractions and round once at the end; skill previews show the rounded cost. Shuriken Throw's cost is multiplied by 1.5 while Twin Shurikens has a rank.
- Triggered skills and procs: proc targets keep their own weapon damage, attack calculation and cast rank, and on-cast/on-attack eligibility no longer depends on damage type or tags. Dragon Knight's Helmet's rank-125 Breath of Fire now procs, Warbringer triggers rank-8 Trigger Finger without triggering itself, and the Blood Demons proc target was repaired. Entity and cooldown cadence, per-projectile non-critical DPS and per-contact ailment inputs were corrected across classes.
- Shared attack calculations include physical and tagged skill damage, tag-based rank bonuses and attack-rating bonuses for every class. Generic projectile damage from items reaches Projectile-tagged skills.
- Angelic augments count the way the game applies them. Damage augments (e.g. Butcher's Fury, Staticshot, Magefury) are their own proc hit, switched on under Procs, instead of adding their damage to the character; timed buffs and conditional bonuses (e.g. Lethal Tempo, Berserker's Rage, Artful Dodger) apply while switched on under Item Blessings, and buffs that proc less often than they last count at their uptime; always-on augments stay as stats. Tempest Strikes, Monstrosity and Avariciousness stack under Combat stacks (Monstrosity's full-stack burst counts for its share of the fill cycle), Magistriker scales with attacks per second, Drunkard Mayhem with active buffs, Minion Mastery speeds up summons instead of the hero, Survivor's Shield counts its immune share of each cooldown, and Zapper (switched on under Procs) adds the lightning its electrocuted targets pass on to nearby monsters. Values, triggers, durations and proc cooldowns follow the game (Weapon Throw, Berserker's Rage, Warsong, Corrosion, Nordic Winter and the spell procs' damage were wrong), spell procs gain their "+X% damage per level of this augment" synergy, and Elementalist now scales with every 100 maximum mana. Longevity raises damage and duration of orbiting skills only, Titan's Power counts its 2 s of every 30 s, Echoes of the Void recasts the skill every 30 s (faster with kills per second), Nordic Winter adds damage, Infinity adds multicast chance, Corrosion stacks enemy damage taken under Combat stacks (10 seconds by default), and Gravity and Warsong reduce damage taken — these augments previously had no effect on the numbers.
- Crushing Blow is calculated. Each proc puts a stack on the target that lasts 3 seconds (a new proc adds one and refreshes the timer), up to 5 stacks plus Maximum Crushing Blow Stacks, and each stack breaks floor(10 × (1 + Crushing Blow Effectiveness / 100))% (12 with one Crusher, 15 with two). The chance rolls once per damage object or projectile and rounds up like the game's integer roll (10.5% acts as 11%). By default the stacks are the average that the main skill's hits per second keep up; Config → Combat Stacks can set the count instead. The stacks join Armor Break (Sundering Strikes included) in one physical break sum that multiplies only the physical part of a hit, after resistance: elemental skills and an attack's converted elemental damage gain nothing. Physical hits of non-attack skills now take Armor Break as well; before, only attacks did. The Stats panel shows Maximum Crushing Blow Stacks and Crushing Blow Armor Break with their sources.
- Attacks lose the fixed 1.5 physical multiplier the planner used to add (the old "crushing blow default"). As in the game, a physical hit takes 1 + (Armor Break + Crushing Blow)/100, so an attack's physical damage without any break is a third lower than before. Converted elemental damage copies the hit before that break: it loses the 1.5 too and no longer gains from Armor Break.
- Physical damage converted to an element (Fire, Cold, Lightning, Poison and Arcane Impact and their small nodes) follows the game. Only the element with the largest total conversion applies, ties going to arcane, cold, fire, lightning, then poison, and the other conversions do nothing; sources of the same element add up. The converted share leaves the physical hit and becomes that element, scaled by its own Skill Damage: 1000 physical with Cold Impact and +40% Cold Skill Damage hits for 500 physical + 700 cold. The converted part takes the element's resistance and breaks, Crushing Blow and Armor Break reach only the physical rest, and skills without physical damage gain nothing. Magic Skill Damage and Total element Skill Damage no longer raise converted damage.
- Ignore Resistance stops at 100% for spells and other non-attack skills too, as it already did for attacks, so stacking it past 100% no longer multiplies damage. Stats show it as 100%, and its breakdown lists the raw total as Overcap (#205).

## Skills

- Butcher: the eight primary attacks use the game's weapon coefficients without invented flat damage, and twenty subtree bonuses enter their own pool at the right stage. Blade Uppercut changes Furious Strike's weapon bonus on a successful attack roll; Meet Daisy adds its damage and critical bonus on every third Brutalizing Slash; Precision Equals Power scales Brutalizing Slash's weapon bonus from Dexterity.
- Butcher buffs and forms: Hellsent, Killing Spree, Rage Trance, Prey on the Weak, Awakening Fury and Ending Fate apply only while their temporary buff is active, with toggles in Config that saves, build codes and loadouts keep. Hunger for Blood needs a bleeding target. Holy Form scales regeneration and life per hit; Insatiable Hunger uses the game's form values. Sacrilegious Scorn and Spiritual Duality need the matching form.
- Butcher critical and attack rating: One Swift Strike, Skullsplitter, Obsidian Blades, Immaculate Butcher and Entry Frenzy add critical bonuses only to their own attack. Bucthering Skill and Sharp Edges multiply only their own skill's attack rating. With 2 Hands is a separate repeat chance; Concussive Blow, Hookstorm and The Real Monster no longer inflate Hook, Chain Swing or passive regeneration.
- Blender: 175% + 15% per rank weapon damage with separate synergy and subtree stages, an 8 s cooldown whose cast cadence uses the reduced cooldown, Nanoblender tags before Blenderang, and an average-contact model for all 14 subskills.
- Blade Strike hits for 100% weapon damage. Its intrinsic critical chance and rank-scaled critical damage apply only to Blade Strike; Blade's Edge, Giant Slayer, Ferocious Swipe and Blade Master modify only those local bonuses.
- Fireball and Storm Bolt use their runtime damage coefficients and separate damage stages. Fireball's Critical Burn adds expected double damage, Orbital Fire casts 4/8/12 bolts, and secondary or rebound damage no longer inflates the main hit. Fire Nova's Traveling Flames area was corrected.
- Amazon: elemental coefficients, weapon bonuses, own-subtree damage pools and movement transformations follow the game, with Powerstasis, third-attack weighting and the Storm Orbs cooldown.
- Maelstrom of Frost lasts 30 s and ticks every second (Intense Temperature shortens the tick). Black Hole lasts 4.5 s (Mass Density extends it) and ticks every 0.4 s. Sand Vortex ticks every 0.33 s for 4 s; Lasting Whirlpool extends it and Accelerated Sand shortens the tick and the lifetime. Soul Spurn hits each enemy once per cast; its 1.25 s ticks exist only while Purgatory keeps the area alive.
- Ten skills such as Storm Dash, Fast Slices, Vault and Link of Sand have the Leap tag again, so Incarnation Leap bonuses reach them; flat Leap damage no longer increases weapon physical damage.
- Unholy Form gives 18.5% + 1.5% per rank damage. Circle of Slugs separates projectiles and waves with a 5.25 s cooldown, Bone Altar adds 8 s to its cooldown, Blood Demons use player recasts, Winter's Bite works for every class, and Arrow Rampage and Wounding Paw subskill coefficients were corrected.

## Incarnation tree

- Elemental Break: universal and matching Break add before multiplying damage, including converted weapon damage; hybrid spells such as Blade Barrier use the on-hit bonus. The five Critical Break nodes (1768/1782/1796/1810/1824) now affect their Break contribution.
- Physical-to-element conversions (1080–1109) add elemental damage from the physical hit, use elemental bonuses and resistance, keep the physical damage and stack beyond 100%.
- Temporal Echo repeats every sixth qualifying spell with its own rank and joins the ailment estimate instead of adding a second damage over time. Death Over Time (813/825) restores 50% or 100% ailment damage against DoT-immune enemies. Manafury (1327) disables only Mana Replenish.
- Auras: damage bonuses use Damaging Aura Effectiveness and other bonuses Buffing Aura Effectiveness. Power of Weakness and Raging Titan increase generic damage; Colossus grants total damage per stack and Combat Mitigation reaches eHP.
- Life and mana conversions apply after the multiplier pass (Vital Power, Strength To Life per 5 Strength). Powerfunneled Lifespan converts after weapon Enhanced Damage, and Asphyxiating Touch keeps hits as the source of ailments with +140% skill damage.
- Shield minimum and maximum damage nodes (1504–1513) stay flat and separate, and shield-gated bonuses reach spells and weapon damage in their own type.
- Many more nodes were corrected: resistance conversions and the 50% cap, full and low life, phasing, regeneration, unarmed, dual axe, shield and staff bonuses, ranged damage from Energy, Charge attribute scaling, Defense, melee and ranged Enhanced Damage, Mind Over Matter, Wizard's Wrath, area radius, bleeding execution and Mechanical Engineering. The Ripper's note lists its dagger requirement.
- Hercules Grip allows two-handed dual wielding only for Swords, Maces and Axes.

## Items

- Boots can roll the "% of Movement Speed Converted to Total Damage Dealt" prefix (Walkers to Dominating, 1-4% up to 9-16%), so items such as Grimwalkers can be rebuilt. The stat is shown on the item but not yet included in damage (#174).
- Signet of Shadows rolls its three Unholy stats from its own pool: attribute conversions to Magic Skill or Attack Damage, Total Faster Cast Rate, Total Attack Speed, sentry and guardian stats and more (#195).
- Crematorium's Pledge gives -30% to Enemy Fire Resistance instead of Ignore Lightning Resistance (#194).
- Ignore Target Defense no longer scales with stars, Increased Defense uses 3% per star, Defense vs Missiles stays separate, and the Increased Defense crystal targets the right stat.
- Rainbow sockets floor each amplified stat (six Ist runes give 132% Magic Find). Star upgrades follow the game's order, add flat ranks and counts for Summon Skills, Sentry Skills and maximum Sentries, and reach random elemental skill bonuses in calculations, tooltips and item text export.
- Pinned implicit rolls match calculations and tooltips, resetting a roll clears it, and starred affix values, item skill rank ranges and mercenary aura ranks can be edited correctly.
- Grimwalkers matches the game: [320-400]% Enhanced Defense, +[30-40] to All Attributes, [30-40]% Magic Skill Damage and +40% Movement Speed instead of fixed or inflated values. Flo's +2% goes to Maximum All Resists, Nju's 3% joins the runeword's -[10-15]% to All Enemy Resistances, Skill Movement Diminish decreased by [40-80]% is added and the stray +64 Defense is gone. Its Demon's Presence proc (level 15-20) appears under Item Blessings in Config. Runewords can now roll ranges, item tooltips list runeword stats in their own Runeword section instead of under Implicit, From Sockets names a runeword's runes without repeating the stats it already includes, and Projectile Speed shows as a flat value (#204).
- St. Tomi's Vibrant Aura: Angel's Vibrance gives percent Increased Life and Mana Replenish instead of flat replenish (#207).
